# A case for beige

Apple went silver in 2001, white in 2003, aluminum in 2006. Somewhere
in that transition we lost **warmth**. A beige Power Mac 6500 in the
afternoon sun is quieter than any modern matte-black enclosure.

> The best computers feel inevitable, not fashionable.
